Plain English: explain a candidate with edge score, risk gate, decay
state, and debate report, in one plain-English research field.

Inputs: a candidate token, its Token DNA, its paper history, and its
LABS edge and risk outputs.
Outputs: a research summary with what is verified, what is missing,
what vetoes, and what to watch next. No buy or sell recommendation
is made by this skill alone.
Limits: advisory only. Intelligence informs, it never gates by itself
and never trades.
Gates: CNS brain paper path and Allen2 bridge live gates stay authoritative.
Sources: Clodds edge/risk, Vibe-research-lab validation/provenance/decay/debate.
