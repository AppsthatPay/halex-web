01Executive summary

On the morning of 24 November 2026, a Solana wallet publicly tagged as belonging to crypto influencer Ansem (Zion Thomas, @blknoiz06) sold roughly 87.5 million ANSEM — his namesake token, The Black Bull — plus holdings in ~123 other tokens, in 877 sales beginning ~08:34 UTC. The ANSEM price fell ~60% within an hour and as much as ~75% at the day's low. The proceeds were bridged to Arbitrum, mostly through Relay, and collected as $2,566,426.53 USDC in wallet 0xAC33748aAd09CEA8E50484Cc0Fa168dc23531B4d. That figure is now stale: at 17:49:52 UTC, $200,000 USDC left the wallet for the first time — routed through Rango (Bridgers) toward a Bitcoin address — leaving $2,366,426.53 USDC as of ~18:10 UTC, 24 November 2026. Circle has not frozen the remaining funds.

Ansem says the wallet was hacked through unauthorised access to his Bullpen trading-terminal profile, which held his imported Solana private key. Several on-chain analysts dispute the hack: the proceeds flowed to a wallet the selling address had previously funded, and the receiving wallet was itself funded with $543,808.79 about 40 minutes before the selling began — a fact this investigation verified on-chain from Etherscan's Arbitrum token-transfer record.
