Skillsets — the proving program
Desk review — how a trade clears

HALEX trades are supervised automation, not manual signing. You set the standing rules once — caps, gates, exit policies — and the desk executes inside them, reviewing every candidate the same way before a single unit moves. If a token meets the bar, the system buys; there is no per-trade trigger for you to pull, and no trade that skips the review.

1 · Gate verdict first

Every candidate is stamped PASS, WATCH, QUARANTINE or FAIL before it can become an order. FAIL and QUARANTINE stop here — no size, no exceptions, and the skip is logged loudly.

2 · Sell simulation at the intended size

The exit is proven before the entry: a real sell is simulated at the size the desk means to hold. Dust out, or under 90% back, is a hard block — a token you can't leave is not a trade.

3 · LP custody, proven on-chain

The pool's position is read on-chain and recomputed to its pool — locker-held or burned passes; deployer-held or unreadable fails closed. Index claims are never taken on faith.

4 · Caps and tag

The order carries its paper/live tag and its exit policy with it, and is checked against the wallet's hard caps — enforced in the executor, unoverridable by any agent or council vote.

5 · Execute, then verify in the wallet

A transaction hash is never confirmation. Proceeds are verified in the wallet — including after a sell, where the balance itself is checked before the trade is called done. PASS positions then go under runner watch: held longer while buy/sell flow and liquidity stay healthy, hard exits always armed.

How security keeps evolving — what to expect
New attacks become new gate tests

Every attack pattern the system meets — a new injection trick, a new impersonation shape — is written up, turned into a gate test, and added to the suite. The Oct 18 record below is what that looks like: clean proposals pass, dirty ones are quarantined, tags stay untouched.

Adversarial self-testing never stops

HALEX red-teams its own fixes on a standing schedule, including overnight rounds. A fix isn't done when it works — it's done when the black-hat pass can't get around it.

Expect fail-closed, on purpose

When something looks wrong, HALEX stops rather than guesses — the Trade Portal already proved that once, correctly, on the $1 B3 round-trip. As monitoring deepens, expect occasional deliberate pauses and more checks asking for your confirmation. A pause is the system protecting you, not breaking.

The layers stay separated

GET TO KNOW HALEX

What HALEX is, how it works, and why it's built the way it is — in plain English. TEMPORARY TAB This orientation page is here while HALEX is young; it will be retired once everyone knows the ropes.

What HALEX is

HALEX is an AI-powered trading and research platform for crypto. It watches every major blockchain at once, investigates tokens before you touch them, runs tested trading skill sets, and keeps your keys on your own device in the HALEX Wallet. Think of it as a whole trading desk — the scanners, the researchers, the traders, the rulebook and the vault — rebuilt as one piece of software with AI woven through it.

What it does
»
Shows you everything

The APEX¹ firehose streams live tokens across Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB and many more chains. Nothing is hidden behind an editor's choice — you filter it yourself.

»
Investigates before you buy

Watch a token and APEX² Research builds the full report: contract, price, liquidity, the buy/sell tape, and what people are saying about it — with an honest verdict and the reasons shown.

»
Trades with proven skill sets

Trading skills run on paper first and are measured honestly. Only a skill that proves itself — 65%+ measured wins over 30+ closed trades — ever earns real money, and you can build your own in the Skills Lab.

»
Mints originals that can't be faked

RUSTY mints a token and its information page in one flow, timestamped at birth — so when the copycats show up, the original is provable.

»
Keeps your money yours

The HALEX Wallet signs on your phone. Keys never leave the device, and HALEX itself never holds your funds.

How it works — one pass, start to finish
1
The firehose comes in

Every new token and trade across the chain catalog flows into one stream. No gatekeeper decides what you're allowed to see.

2
Research checks it

APEX² Research scores structure, liquidity and identity, and screens for the impersonation scams that dominate new launches.

3
The brain decides

The Central Nervous System weighs the evidence and makes the call: what to buy or sell, which wallet, what size, and which exit plan.

4
The switchboard routes it

One dispatcher sends the order to the right wallet — and if that wallet is busy or capped, to a free wallet on the same chain. The exit plan travels with the trade.

5
Wallets just execute

Wallets are hands, not brains: they do exactly what they're told, inside hard spending caps they cannot override, and your HALEX Wallet signs on-device.

6
Every trade teaches the system

Win or lose, each closed trade gets an autopsy. The lessons are filed in the Braintree, so the skill sets get sharper instead of repeating mistakes.

The parts, by name
The Central Nervous System

The brain plus the switchboard. It takes in everything — feeds, signals, research — and makes every call. One brain, one ledger of truth, so no trade is ever doubled or lost. If the brain goes quiet, wallets stop opening new trades and simply manage the exits they already hold: it fails safe, never wild.

The Braintree Database

HALEX's living knowledgebase. Winning skill sets hang there, categorized like branches, ready to be cloned and improved. Everything in it is a versioned observation with its source attached — knowledge with receipts, not rumors.

BrainData

The flow of knowledge into the Braintree — trade autopsies, research findings, hard lessons. BrainData is what the system learns; the Braintree is where what it learned lives. Every piece of BrainData enters through a single guarded doorway and passes two security gates before it's allowed to become knowledge.

DRUM FILED SKILL

A body of hard-won trading knowledge, retired from active duty and filed as a skill in the Braintree. DRUM doesn't run anymore — nothing to crash, nothing to drift. Its knowledge is simply there, clean and retrievable, whenever a skill set or a research question needs it.

SMT

The Skills Lab builder. SMT helps you create skill sets and keeps the ones you make organized — it's the patient librarian of the Lab, and the partner you talk to in the Skill Forge while your ideas become a working skill.

The CIA micro-processors

A crew of small, single-job specialists that work for the brain: one watches the feed, one reads the tape, one screens for copycat scams, one sizes trades, one manages exits, one writes the autopsies. Each does one thing, reports back, and never freelances — small processors, tightly supervised.

APEX² Research

The head of research. It runs the investigations — token reports, wallet traces, scam screening — and feeds the machine facts and finished research. Only its findings cross into the app; you see the report, the verdict and the reasons, never a black box.

halex-security

Two gates — one stops malicious content aimed at the screens, one stops injection aimed at the machine — standing guard over everything written into the Braintree. Security answers to nobody in the trading system. Details and the live monitor are on the Security tab.

The HALEX Wallet

Where your money actually lives. A multi-wallet vault on your phone — hundreds of wallets, protected by a 2-of-3 multisig design and an app lock — that signs every trade on-device. HALEX can prepare anything; only your wallet can approve it.

The skill-set system — logic and reasoning, not just rules

A HALEX skill set is more than a list of if-then rules. Each skill carries its reasoning: what it's looking for, why that pattern makes money, when it refuses to play, and how it gets out. That's what "logic/reasoning skill sets" means here — the skill can explain itself, and its score card shows its trades so you can judge the reasoning against the record.

Skills are versioned modules filed in the Braintree. Winners keep trading and get cloned. Losers aren't deleted or defended — they're reformulated from the autopsy evidence and re-proven on paper. Changes happen at decision boundaries, never in the middle of an open trade. And with the Skill Forge in the Skills Lab, you can design your own the same way: talk it through, get the tactical breakdown, forge it, paper-prove it.

Software built with AI — and why that's the smart move

HALEX's software is hardwired: fixed, tested, immutable. The AI never edits the app — it connects through sockets and operates it, like an electrician working a panel rather than rebuilding the house. That split is the whole trick. The hardwired core gives you safety and predictability: the wallet can't be talked into sending your keys anywhere, because no socket exists that could. The AI layer gives you leverage: research that reads a thousand tokens, skill sets tuned from every autopsy, a research head that never sleeps.

Markets move at machine speed and drown humans in data. A person can't watch twenty chains, screen every launch for copycats, and re-check every exit plan at 3 a.m. The AI can — while the hardwired rules make sure it can only act inside the lines you've approved. Speed where speed helps, stubbornness where safety demands it.

Why a game engine — Unity — renders the 3D world

The Chaintarium — HALEX's 3D map of the blockchain world, where Bitcoin is the sun, chains are planets and wallets, pools and tokens live on their surfaces — is a lot of graphics for a phone. So it gets its own dedicated GPU layer: a game engine, Unity, whose only job is drawing that world smoothly. The app itself stays light and fast for trading, while the engine does the heavy lifting it's built for — rendering thousands of objects at a steady frame rate on the graphics chip instead of the main processor. Smart division of labor: the trading app never slows down because the universe is pretty, and the universe never stutters because the app is busy.

How a HALEX swap is built
One quote, every pool

When you ask for a quote, HALEX asks a router to search nearly every Solana liquidity pool at once and bring back the best route — sometimes splitting your trade across several pools when that gets you a better price. The quote on this page is that live answer, not an estimate typed in advance.

The fee rides inside the trade

HALEX charges 0.8% on a swap, and it is taken inside the same transaction as the swap itself — one approval moves everything at once. That means the fee can never be taken without the swap happening: if any part fails, the whole transaction unwinds and nothing moves, fee included.

Everything is priced before you confirm

The quote shows the full cost up front: the network fee Solana charges (a fraction of a cent), the HALEX fee, and the net amount you receive — one all-in number. Confirming re-checks that number; you never approve a swap and find the cost afterwards.

Straight to your wallet

The tokens you receive settle directly into the wallet you swapped from — HALEX never holds them. The HALEX fee is collected by the network into HALEX's own fee accounts as part of the transaction, visible on-chain like everything else here.

Fail-closed, on purpose

Before anything is signed, the trade is simulated. If the price has moved beyond your slippage tolerance, the swap stops rather than filling at a worse price — the same rule the Trade Portal proved on its $1 round-trip, where it refused once, correctly, before succeeding.
