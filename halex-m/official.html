Inside HALEX-M

APEX2 Research scorecards carry Token DNA for every token: type, birth launchpad and pool, deployer lineage, funding tracebacks, LP custody, and a copycat family census. Chaintarium shows the chains as a 3D world. War Chest holds the account tools. The large-send tax tiers and the wallet tab rebuild are decided and staged, and ship with the next HALEX-M work.
