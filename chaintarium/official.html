Chaintarium

The official Chaintarium page. Built by HALEX.

Chaintarium is the 3D chain view built by HALEX: every chain is a world, with lifespan-graded planets, cityscapes, and a spaceship HUD. Tap a planet and the HUD locks on and shows what the build already knows. It lives inside HALEX-M.

Honest state, November 22, 2026

The scale build is live and verified (index md5 189cbc803a1004d5aa6ff413e6c32221). The activity the worlds show is a simulated model, not a live feed. The HUD shows only data the build already has: name, symbol, type, and orbit. No market data is invented.

First use: our dates

  • November 13, 2026: first verified live browser test: the 3D planet view renders with no engine error. The same test found the live copy still loading Three.js from a CDN.
  • November 14, 2026: the lifespan pass landed: Active, Dying, and Dead worlds graded by color and light, with city models and ruins. Gamification was proposed the same day and deferred by the founder.
  • November 19 to 20, 2026: the sunlit overhaul went live: a warm Bitcoin sun with corona and varied worlds.
  • November 20, 2026: the HUD pass landed in the sandbox: floating name labels removed, tap-to-lock green HUD markers with a 4 second readout.
  • November 21, 2026: the scale and camera build verified 104 to 0 in the sandbox and went live on Pages (index md5 189cbc803a1004d5aa6ff413e6c32221).

Chaintarium Research System

The Chaintarium Research System is the research terminal inside HALEX-M, not a separate app: it is the same 3D view plus the research layer that reads each world. It has no separate live surface, so it is documented here, on the Chaintarium page, and not given its own page.

The scale law

Chaintarium follows the original web Chaintarium's scale: phone tilt, zoom, and move must work. The November 21 scale build fixed the phone view after the founder judged the sunlit build horrible on the wrong scale.
