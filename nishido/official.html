Nishido

The official Nishido page. Built by HALEX.

Nishido is the 1v1 swordsman game built by HALEX on Unity: a realistic third-person duel, a card system that plays out as one choreographed fight, kanji flashes on deadly blows, no blood, torn clothing only, and eight arenas from a Japanese garden to a snowy mountain field.

A full rebuild is in progress. The live build is still the old one, and this page says so plainly.

Honest state, November 22, 2026

The live build's menus, card stacking, and fight flow were judged broken on the founder's phone on November 21, 2026. The rebuild was ordered the same day: the card presentation, stacking UI, fight rendering, menus, and scenery rebuilt, with drag-and-drop deck building and choreographed stack-played fights from studied skeletal strike data. Phase 1 (research, UI rebuild, fighters, arenas, URP prep) is done on disk. The Editor phase is running as of November 22, 2026. The AI versus AI mode stays secret.
