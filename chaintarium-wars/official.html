Chaintarium Wars

The official Chaintarium Wars page. Built by HALEX.

Chaintarium Wars is the gamified Chaintarium, designed by HALEX. Claim planets, call revive or dead on dying worlds, and keep an explorer logbook. Sign-in is by wallet, and ownership comes from buying your first planet dome and ships.

Chaintarium Wars is design stage only. No build exists yet, and this page says so plainly.

Honest state, November 22, 2026

There is no Chaintarium Wars build and no game link. The design was proposed on November 14, 2026 and deferred the same day by the founder. The buy-in (10 dollars for 30 days per chain) was decided on November 19, 2026. Per-chain receiving addresses, per-chain pricing rules, and lapse and renewal behavior are still open and no addresses have been supplied or invented.

First use: our dates

  • November 14, 2026: gamification proposed: lifespan prediction, planet claiming, explorer logbook, rescue runs, seasons and leaderboards. The founder deferred it the same day: no direction chosen, no design doc or build authorized.
  • November 16, 2026: the public Chaintarium Wars Telegram group was ordered created.
  • November 19, 2026: the buy-in decided: 10 dollars for 30 days per chain, one simple contract per planet and wallet buy.

What is decided

Sign-in is by wallet. Your home planet and player kind come from the system you settle. Factions render from that. The Bitcoin sun is unclaimable. What is still open: per-chain receiving addresses, per-chain pricing, lapse and renewal behavior, whether ships are included or separate, and sequencing against Machine Warrior.
