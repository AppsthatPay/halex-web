Chaintarium Wars

The gamified Chaintarium. Designed by HALEX.

Chaintarium Wars is the gamified Chaintarium, designed by HALEX. Claim planets, call revive or dead on dying worlds, and keep an explorer logbook. Sign-in is by wallet, and ownership comes from buying your first planet dome and ships.

Honest state, November 24, 2026

Chaintarium Wars is design stage only. No build exists yet, and this page says so plainly. The design was proposed on November 14, 2026 and deferred the same day by the founder. The buy-in (10 dollars for 30 days per chain) was decided on November 19, 2026.
