APEX2 is the research and scoring layer built by HALEX. Every token scorecard carries Token DNA: type, birth launchpad and pool, deployer lineage, funding tracebacks, LP custody, and a copycat family census. It says what it could not verify, gives no score where there is none to give, and tells no fairy tale.

Honest state, November 24, 2026

APEX2 lives inside HALEX-M and TrenchSwap. It has no standalone app of its own. A scorecard lists what is verified and what is not. Where a field is unverified it stays NOT READ. APEX2 never calls a token safe.
