APEX2 is the research and scoring layer built by HALEX. Every token scorecard carries Token DNA: type, birth launchpad and pool, deployer lineage, funding tracebacks, LP custody, and a copycat family census. It says what it could not verify, gives no score where there is none to give, and tells no fairy tale.

APEX2 lives inside HALEX-M and TrenchSwap. It has no standalone app of its own.

How to read an APEX2 scorecard

A scorecard lists what is verified and what is not. Mint authority, freeze authority, holders, liquidity, and the copycat census are each shown with their source. Where a field is unverified it stays NOT READ. APEX2 never calls a token safe.
