# Multi-Perspective Dialectic Engine (MPDE)

## What it does
Takes any claim or proposition and tests it across three distinct lenses (empirical grounding, logical consistency, and adversarial skepticism) to eliminate blind spots and synthesize a balanced conclusion.

## How it was tested
Tested via `test_dialectic_engine.py` covering supported empirical cases, absolute quantifier adversarial traps, and empty input validation.

## What it does NOT do
- Does not guarantee absolute objective truth.
- Does not replace human domain expertise in complex epistemological debates.
