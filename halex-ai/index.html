How HALEX-AI Learns

When HALEX-AI does not know a thing, the design is: ping the SEEDS in the matching ZINIUM categories first, then harvest the open internet with its own reasoning, learn, and add the knowledge to ZINIUM.

Every person's use grows the knowledge. Only knowledge is kept: never personal information, and never what the person was trying to do. A ZINIUM memory of a thing is what it is, what it does, and what it looks like, plus a thumbnail ID for positive identification.

The design is set. On this public page the live harvest loop is not wired in yet, and it is listed under Not Built Yet below, in plain sight.
