Machine Warrior

The official Machine Warrior page. Built by HALEX.

Machine Warrior is the assassin-droid game built by HALEX on Unity. You play the AS-7, a sleek armored assassin droid in matte black with a red visor, built for agility. The enemy remembers and co-evolves with you, and the endgame is fighting your choice of AI through the socket.

Honest state, November 22, 2026

The live build still carries the pointer-lock crash on phones: the game asks for pointer lock, which a phone cannot give, and play stalls. The fix is written (touch look uses touch-drag instead of pointer lock) and the pipeline is ready, queued behind the Nishido rebuild because Unity work runs one project at a time. The AS-7 remodel v2 is built and awaits the founder's verdict.

The core design law

Absorption progression: the droid absorbs what it defeats and grows. The enemy is a co-evolving remembering opponent, never impossible. The endgame is fighting your choice of AI via the socket. Art first, always: beautiful games, not chunky slow lame graphics.
