NOC

The official NOC page. Built by HALEX.

NOC is the night-vision rodent-shooting game built by HALEX on Unity. Real night-vision and thermal optics, four optic modes, rodents that move with purpose, and messy sculpted scenes: trash dumps, silos, barns, and orchard groves. A serious adult shooter, marketed to players 21 and up.

The rules NOC runs on

Rodents are inquisitive and move with purpose: food search, mate, fight, hide, sniff, taste, interact. They hug edges and walls, freeze, then bolt. Deaths are realistic but gore is never shown. Squirrels never glow: they have no tapetum, so they never show eyeshine, and that is the tell. Thermal eyes are always dark pits.
