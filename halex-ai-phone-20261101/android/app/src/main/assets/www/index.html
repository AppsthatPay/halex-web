It aids the reply's processing trace only; answers still come from the chain records, and it never decides, executes, signs, spends, or trades.

ZINIUM link — phone to phone

    
Plain truth. Packet sending over WiFi or
    Bluetooth is not available on this phone yet. No packet-sending
    transport and no Bluetooth bridge exist in this build, so this tab
    sends nothing on its own and lists no nearby phones. What works
    today is the manual path below: you pick a saved endpoint, prepare
    a chain hello and a packet, send them yourself through your own
    share sheet or by copying, and the other phone pastes them in and
    explicitly accepts. Received packets land pending-review only —
    nothing is adopted automatically.

Saved endpoint (typed by you)

      
Only an endpoint you type in and save here — or
      one you already saved in the WiFi Relay tab — can be picked.
      Picking one does not connect to anything by itself, and endpoints
      are never invented here.

      
      
      
      
      
      
      

    
Chain hello — step 2

      
A hello is the manifest summary only. Preparing
      it makes no network send — send it yourself through your own
      share sheet or by copying.

      
      

    
Acceptance — step 3

      
No packet can be prepared until the receiving
      phone explicitly accepts. Tap below only when the other phone
      has accepted.

      

    
Packet — step 4

      
A packet is a record summary only: packet id,
      timestamp, manifest summary, record counts. No personal data, no
      network name, no endpoint inside it. Preparing it makes no
      network send — transfer it yourself the same way.

      
      

    
Send packet — step 4b (internet, your endpoint)

      
Once a packet is prepared and an endpoint is
      picked, you can send this one packet to that endpoint over
      your own joined connection — one send, no retry, only when
      you press send here. This is the internet leg, not Bluetooth:
      no Bluetooth bridge exists in this build. The manual
      copy/share-sheet path above still works unchanged.

      
      

    
Receive on this phone — step 5

      
Paste a packet the other phone sent you, then
      tap accept. It lands pending-review for your approval — approve
      it into the chain records, or reject it. Nothing is adopted
      automatically.
